# The checklist, in chat

The last step of every run. It turns the intake into the recruiter's next actions, with dates, and stays open in the chat so the recruiter can come back and build on it.

## The list

Fill in names, channels and dates from the transcript and the summary. Dates are real calendar dates (work out the weekday). Leave out a line that does not apply, for example calibration when none ran.

**Today**
- ☐ Post the intake notes in [channel] (the Slack post is drafted above)
- ☐ Send the JD draft to [HM] for sign-off
- ☐ Send the calibration profiles to [scorers] to score by [date, within 24 hours]

**This week**
- ☐ Book the persona interview with [named person]: 20 to 25 minutes on why they joined, what keeps them there, what frustrates them and the language they use about the work. Add what the intake left open.
- ☐ Get [HM] to sign off the screen questions
- ☐ Get the job live on [ATS or careers page] and save the link
- ☐ Sourcing goal: 20 candidates identified and contacted by [date, five working days after the intake]

**In your diary**
- ☐ Sourcing blocks: a few hours a week, booked as recurring time
- ☐ Friday update: time every Friday for the weekly update to [the client's Head of Talent], copying your Move lead
- ☐ First interviews: it's a good idea to hold time in [HM]'s diary from two weeks out, so you have slots ready to book candidates into. We recommend 3 to 5 slots to start with, so you can drive towards a solid shortlist.

**Open questions**
- ☐ One line per "needed before sourcing" question from the summary: the question, the owner, the date

## Running it

1. Show the list in one message, grouped as above.
2. Ask the recruiter to tap what's already done, using tappable multi-select questions where the session supports them (one question per group, four options at most each; split a group that runs longer). Add "or just type what's done".
3. For everything left, offer the help that fits:
   - **Diary blocks:** with Google Calendar connected, create the sourcing blocks and the Friday update in the recruiter's own calendar after they confirm the times.
   - **HM interview slots:** draft the message asking the HM to hold 3 to 5 slots. With the client's calendar connected and the recruiter's go-ahead, create the holds as tentative events the HM can move.
   - **Chasers:** draft a short message for each open question, addressed to its owner.
   - **JD and screen sign-off:** draft the note to the HM that goes with each document.
4. Show the updated list after each round, with finished items ticked (☑). End with: "Come back to this chat any time and I'll pick the list up from here."

Always show the recruiter anything before it is sent or booked in someone else's name.
