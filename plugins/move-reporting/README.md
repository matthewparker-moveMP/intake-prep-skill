# Move Reporting

Friday reporting for Move's embedded recruiters.

- **friday-hm-update** writes the end of week update for one role, for the hiring manager in that role's hiring channel. Run it once per role.
- **friday-hot-update** rolls up the week's hiring manager updates into one update for the client's Head of Talent, with Matt copied. It asks follow-ups on priority roles: close dates, risks to closing and what raises the odds.

The standard behind both skills lives in Notion: Recruiter Onboarding > 3. Your client and your account > Your Friday updates. When the format changes, update the Notion page and this plugin together.

## Connectors

Slack is optional. When it's connected, friday-hot-update finds the week's hiring manager posts itself, and both skills can post on the recruiter's confirmation. Without Slack, recruiters paste the text in.

## Tip

Dictate your notes using the microphone in Claude or Wispr Flow. The skills do the structuring.
