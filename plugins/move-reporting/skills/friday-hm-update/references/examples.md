# Hiring manager update: worked examples

Both examples use invented clients and candidates.

## Example 1: a strong week

```
*Account Executive, DACH: end of week update, 9 Oct*
🎥 Loom walkthrough (3 mins)

*Summary:* Strong week, with two candidates at final and a third through to challenge. I need your feedback on three new profiles by Tuesday to keep the next wave moving.

*Pipeline:*
• Final (2): Lena Vogt and Jonas Brandt, both with Sophie on Thursday. Lena is at offer stage elsewhere, so a quick decision helps.
• Challenge (1): Mia Keller, case study due Monday.
• HM interview (3): Tobias Wirth, Clara Hahn and Felix Roth, all booked for next week.
• HM review (3): awaiting your feedback, links below.
• Recruiter screen (6): 4 booked, 2 waiting to book.

*Key moves this week:*
• Screened 7 candidates, 3 progressed to HM review
• 45 candidates contacted on outbound, 9 replies

*What the market is telling us:*
• Mid-market AEs in Berlin expect €75k to €85k base, so our €80k top sits well.
• Three candidates asked about the commission cap. A clear answer on accelerators lands better on first calls.

*Focus for next week:*
• Close out finals, with a decision on Lena by Friday
• Source 20 more candidates with SaaS mid-market experience

*Actions required from you:*
• Review 3 profiles by Tuesday: [Profile 1], [Profile 2], [Profile 3]
• Confirm the accelerator structure so I can share it on calls

Let me know if you have any questions and have a great weekend!
```

## Example 2: a stuck role

```
*Senior Backend Engineer: end of week update, 9 Oct*

*Summary:* Screens are up, but few are converting because the band sits below what strong Go engineers earn. I need a decision on flexing the band to keep this search on track.

*Pipeline:*
• HM interview (1): Arjun Mehta, with Daniel on Wednesday.
• HM review (2): awaiting your feedback, links below.
• Recruiter screen (8): 6 booked, 2 waiting to book.

*Key moves this week:*
• Screened 9 candidates, 2 progressed to HM review
• 60 candidates contacted on outbound, 7 replies
• Shifted sourcing toward fintech and payments, where Go use is highest

*What the market is telling us:*
• Four of the nine expect £105k to £115k base. Our top is £95k.
• Two strong candidates withdrew over five days in the office. Three days would widen the pool.

*Focus for next week:*
• Screen 10 and get two more to HM interview
• Test outreach at £110k, if you approve it

*Actions required from you:*
• Decision on moving the band to £110k by Wednesday
• Review 2 profiles: [Profile 1], [Profile 2]
• Your view on three days in the office for this role

Let me know if you have any questions and have a great weekend!
```
