# Setup and guide wording

The wording the skill uses with the recruiter: the setup message, connection and browser steps, the handover walkthrough and the calibration delivery. The main instructions set the order; this file holds the words. Adapt everything to the client, the role and what the recruiter already said.

## Tone for everything the recruiter reads

- Plain, warm, short. One idea per sentence.
- Explain a Move term the first time it appears:
  - **calibration profiles:** "3 or 4 real LinkedIn profiles you show the hiring manager, so you agree what good looks like before you start sourcing"
  - **persona interview:** "a 20-minute chat with a strong person already in the team, so your outreach sounds like the real job"
- Name things by what the recruiter sees on screen ("the browser panel", "Connectors"). Keep tool names, file paths and technical detail out of recruiter-facing messages.
- Write in positive constructions, including when something is unavailable: say what happens instead.

## The setup message (one message)

Drop any line the request already answered, and confirm what you have instead.

> I'll help you prep for your intake with Sam. I'll research Northwind and build you a Word prep pack to read before the call and keep open during it, then find calibration profiles in a second file. I'll tell you what I'm doing as I go.
>
> **Here's how we'll do this:**
> 1. **Setup** (we're here): what I need and what's connected
> 2. **The business and the team:** what Northwind is trying to achieve this year, and what this team has to deliver. I'll check my read with you before moving on.
> 3. **The role:** what the role really is and the questions that matter
> 4. **Your prep pack:** a Word document, plus a walkthrough of how to run the call. You can start reading it straight away.
> 5. **Calibration profiles:** real LinkedIn profiles to show Sam, in a second file. This is the longest step, so it runs while you read the pack.
>
> **A few quick things:**
> - Do you have the job description? Paste it, drop the file in here, share a link, or tell me it's still to be written.
> - Anything else from Northwind I should read? Org charts, strategy decks, old job specs, onboarding docs, and the context pack Move shared with you. Drop them straight in.
>
> **What's connected**
> - **Ready to use:** Gmail and Google Calendar, so I can pick up your thread with Sam and the invite, plus web search for public research.
> - **Worth connecting:** Northwind's knowledge base or shared drive, so I can read their goals and team pages directly. Tell me if you'd like to connect it and I'll walk you through it.
>
> **The browser for LinkedIn**
> [the browser section below]
>
> I'll build from the job description, your email and calendar, anything you drop in, and public research on Northwind. Reply with anything extra, or just say "go" and I'll start.

What each source adds, in the recruiter's terms, most valuable first:

| Source | What it adds to the prep |
| --- | --- |
| The client's knowledge base or shared drive (Confluence, Notion, Google Drive, SharePoint) | Company goals, team pages, org charts, past job specs: the best material for the business and team sections |
| A browser signed in to LinkedIn | Calibration profiles and a picture of who's in the team today |
| Gmail and Google Calendar | The client thread, the meeting time and who's attending |
| The client's Slack | How the team talks about its work and priorities |
| Move's context pack | Move's notes on the account: dropped into the chat as a file |

## Connecting a tool

Give these steps only for tools the recruiter asks to connect:

> Click **+** at the bottom left of this chat, go to **Connectors**, then **Manage connectors**. Click **+**, pick the tool, and sign in. For a client's tool, sign in with your client login. Then click **+** again, go to **Connectors**, and switch the new tool on for this chat.
>
> For a tool the list lacks, ask your Move lead to add it for the team.

Whenever a client system is involved, add: "Only connect client systems you've been given access to for your work with them."

## The browser for LinkedIn

**When a browser is available** (the app's built-in browser or Claude in Chrome), open linkedin.com and check the recruiter is signed in. When they are, list it under *Ready to use*: "a browser signed in to LinkedIn, so I can find calibration profiles", and skip the browser section. When they're signed out, ask them to sign in in the browser panel and tell you when it's done.

**When no browser is available,** offer the app's built-in browser first:

> To find calibration profiles I look at LinkedIn through a browser. Setting it up takes about 5 minutes, and you only do it once.
>
> The easiest option is the browser inside this app, which runs right here. I'll open LinkedIn in the browser panel on the right. The first time, you can import your logins from Chrome in one click, or sign in to LinkedIn yourself. It remembers you after that.
>
> Set it up now, or leave it for this intake? (Tap an option, or just type your answer.)

**Which browser to offer.** When the recruiter says yes, try opening LinkedIn in the built-in browser. When the built-in browser tools are missing from the session, or opening it fails (it may be switched off for the team or still rolling out), offer Claude in Chrome:

> Let's use Chrome instead. It takes a few more steps:
> 1. Open Google Chrome and go to the Chrome Web Store. Search for **Claude** and click **Add to Chrome**.
> 2. Sign in with your Claude account when it asks.
> 3. Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Claude, so it stays visible.
> 4. Accept the permissions it asks for.
> 5. Back in this app, click your initials at the bottom left, then **Settings**, then **Connectors**. Find **Claude in Chrome**, click **Configure**, and switch it on.
> 6. Check you're signed in to LinkedIn in Chrome.
>
> Tell me when that's done and I'll check it's working.

**When the recruiter leaves it for later:**

> Got it, we'll leave calibration profiles for next time. They're real LinkedIn profiles you show the hiring manager, so you both agree what good looks like in about ten minutes, which makes them one of the most useful parts of an intake. It's worth setting up before your next one.

Then run steps 2 to 4 as normal. The pack's calibration section carries one spoken question in place of the profiles: "Who's someone you'd hire tomorrow, and what makes them right?"

**During sourcing:** when LinkedIn asks the recruiter to confirm who they are, pause, ask them to complete the check in the browser, and carry on once they reply. Keep searches focused so LinkedIn stays happy.

## Starting the research (after the recruiter replies)

> Thanks. I'm building from the job description, your thread with Sam, the invite and public research on Northwind. Starting now.
>
> **Step 2 of 5: The business and the team.** [what you're doing, and why it matters]

## Handover with the prep pack (step 4)

Deliver the pack as soon as it's built, then walk the recruiter through the call. Adapt to the pack and the meeting length:

> **Step 4 of 5: Your prep pack.** Here it is. Read it once before the call; it takes about 10 minutes. Here's how to use it on a 60-minute call:
>
> 1. **Open (2 to 3 minutes).** Say how you'd like to run it: the business first, then the team, then the role, then the practical details. Ask if it's OK to record.
> 2. **The business (about 6 minutes).** Read back my guess about what Northwind needs this year and ask Sam to put it in their own words. Let them talk, and follow up on anything big ("how fast?", "compared with what?").
> 3. **The team (about 6 minutes).** Check the second guess, then ask why this hire is happening now.
> 4. **The role (about 35 minutes).** Work through the role questions. Then share your screen and show the calibration profiles one at a time: yes, no or maybe, and why.
> 5. **The close (about 10 minutes).** Pay and what you can tell candidates about it, location and the interview process. Then read back the pitch at the end of the pack and ask whether it lands. Agree next steps with dates, and book the persona interview.
>
> After the call, come back with the transcript and say "log the Northwind intake". I'll write up the intake notes for Sam, draft the JD and your screen, and give you a checklist of next steps. If you use Google Meet, turn on note-taking with Gemini and the notes land in your Google Drive. If you use Granola, export the notes and drop them in.
>
> **Next, step 5 of 5:** I'm finding calibration profiles on LinkedIn. It's the longest step, so start reading the pack and I'll post the profiles here when they're ready. Keep the Claude app open while I search.

When the recruiter left the browser for later, replace the last paragraph with: "That's everything for this intake. Set up the browser before your next one and I'll add calibration profiles."

## Delivering the calibration profiles (step 5)

> **Step 5 of 5: Calibration profiles.** Here are 4 profiles to show Sam, with a note on the question each one tests. Keep this file open next to the prep pack for the role part of the call.
>
> That's everything for this intake. Good luck with the call.
