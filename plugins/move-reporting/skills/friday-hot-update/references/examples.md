# Head of Talent update: worked examples

Both examples use invented clients and candidates.

## Example 1: a good week

```
*Halden: end of week update, 9 Oct*

*Summary:* Good week across the board, with two AE DACH candidates at final and both priority roles moving. Backend is the one to watch, and a decision on the band from Daniel would move it forward.

*This week in numbers:*
• 26 calls · 155 outbound contacted · 10 submitted to HM · 4 roles live

*By role:*
• Account Executive, DACH (priority): 7 screened, 3 submitted, 43%, 45 outbound
• Senior Backend Engineer (priority): 9 screened, 2 submitted, 22% ⚠️, 60 outbound
• Customer Success Manager: 6 screened, 3 submitted, 50%, 30 outbound
• Product Designer: 4 screened, 2 submitted, 50%, 20 outbound

*Priority roles:*
*Account Executive, DACH:* Lena Vogt and Jonas Brandt at final with Sophie on Thursday. On track for an offer by 17 Oct.
• Risk to closing: Lena has a competing offer, decision due 16 Oct.
• What raises the odds: Sophie's feedback within a day of Thursday, and a clear answer on accelerators before the offer.

*Senior Backend Engineer:* One at HM interview, two awaiting review. Conversion is low because the band sits £10k to £20k below market.
• Risk to closing: strong candidates withdrawing at screen over pay and five days in the office.
• What raises the odds: the band at £110k and three days in the office for this role.

*Blockers:* Backend band decision with Daniel, needed by Wednesday.
*What's working:* Sharing the case study brief at screen means AE candidates arrive at challenge well prepared. Two of the last three passed first time. Worth trying on CSM.
*Focus for next week:* 25 calls, 150 outbound, an offer out on AE DACH and two more Backend candidates to HM interview.
*Actions required from you:*
• Back the Backend band decision with Daniel by Wednesday
• Ask Sophie for same-day feedback after Thursday's finals

Have a great weekend!
```

## Example 2: a priority hire at risk

```
*Ostara: end of week update, 9 Oct*

*Summary:* A slower week, with our lead Head of Product candidate at risk from a competing process. I need your help getting her a final with the CPO by Wednesday.

*This week in numbers:*
• 14 calls · 90 outbound contacted · 4 submitted to HM · 3 roles live

*By role:*
• Head of Product (priority): 3 screened, 1 submitted, 33% ⚠️ falling for a second week, 25 outbound
• Data Analyst: 5 screened, 2 submitted, 40%, 30 outbound
• Sales Development Rep: 6 screened, 1 submitted, 17% ⚠️, 35 outbound

*Priority roles:*
*Head of Product:* Priya Shah is our lead candidate after a strong second round. The final with the CPO is still to book. Close expected early November once the final happens next week.
• Risk to closing: Priya is at final stage elsewhere, decision expected 20 Oct. The CPO's diary is full until 23 Oct.
• What raises the odds: a 45-minute final with the CPO by Wednesday, and early sight of the package so we can test numbers with Priya before the final.

*Blockers:* SDR conversion. Applicant volume is high, and 1 in 6 screened is progressing. Agreeing tighter criteria with Tom on Monday (prior outbound quota experience) would lift it.
*What's working:* Posting the Data Analyst take-home brief upfront filtered applicants well, and conversion held at 40%.
*Focus for next week:* 20 calls, 120 outbound, Priya through final, SDR criteria agreed and two SDRs to HM.
*Actions required from you:*
• 45 minutes with the CPO for Priya by Wednesday
• The Head of Product package range by Tuesday

Have a great weekend!
```
