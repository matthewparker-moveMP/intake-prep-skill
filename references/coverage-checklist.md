# Coverage checklist — what a complete intake captures

A complete intake answers everything below. Use it two ways:

- **PREP:** every item is either pre-filled from research (and marked "confirm on call") or carried as a question. This is the coverage floor for the question set.
- **LOG:** the gap check scores the transcript against this list, section by section, and drives the persona-interview recommendation.

The list is derived from shipped searches; every item has earned its place by being needed downstream.

## 1. The role brief

- Business problem in the HM's own words (one sentence, quotable)
- New head or backfill, and the org story (e.g. "a generalist role split in two; this is the research half")
- Reporting line and team shape, with names and sizes ("reports to Roman, ~5–6 analysts; works daily with Sebastian's ~7 engineers")
- Relationship with the rest of the business: who the role serves, who they answer to day to day, where the remit stops and adjacent functions begin, and the internal-service vs external-revenue split where relevant
- Success at 6 and 12 months
- The day-to-day work, concretely — and the scope boundaries: what this role is NOT (the not-statements do heavy lifting downstream: "not dashboards/BI, not ML-research, not financial-services fraud")
- Interview process step by step, with owners and typical total timeline
- Location and hybrid pattern, precisely (which days, how many, any remote weeks)
- Comp: band, ceiling behaviour, negotiation flex, who negotiates ("co-founder involved in offers"), supplements (relocation bonus, one-time payments)
- Visa and relocation package in candidate-safe terms

## 2. The candidate profile

- Hard gates vs strong preferences, each offer-tested
- The identity axis in the client's words (e.g. "analyst, not Data Scientist" — the decisive rejection axis)
- The domain gate: hard requirement, tiebreaker, or proxy — and the named wrong-domain
- Target titles: strong signal / adjacent-validate / exclude outright
- Target companies: tiered hypothesis with client reaction per tier; adjacencies flagged untested vs validated; exclusions (off-limits companies, DNC)
- Geography as a cascade with pass order, and the relocation stance per pass
- Seniority reality: years as bar or buffer; comp-ceiling behaviour (which profile shapes price out)
- Education signals (fuzzy flag vs mandatory)
- Edge buckets: which are in-scope-but-flagged, and *who must align* before heavy outreach on them
- Open questions with named owners and dates

## 3. Calibration

- A reaction per starter-set profile: yes/no/maybe and the *why*, verbatim where possible
- Boundary resolutions from the edge cases (each edge profile tested a named question — record the verdict)
- The 30-second yes and the swipe-past, described
- Named scorer(s) at the client and who the de-facto technical bar is
- Where past candidates for this or similar roles failed
- Do-not-contact list (the client's own DNC plus any off-limits companies)
- Who scores the follow-up calibration sheet and the deadline

## 4. Persona and outreach language

The intake carries persona-lite; the gap check decides whether a separate interview is needed.

- Who currently does this work well (interviewee nomination + intro agreed on the call)
- Motivators in concrete terms (what energises the best person they've seen)
- Frustrations that make this person disengage or leave (every frustration is a messaging flip)
- The language of the craft — phrases worth quoting in outreach
- Work environment truths: what the team actually feels like
- What stops good people at competitor companies from moving (comfort, comp inertia, relocation friction) and the honest counters
- Interviewee proximity note: how close the voices are to the exact target (flag when neither interviewee is the target — it caps how hard the persona can be leaned on)

**Persona-interview trigger:** recommend the separate 20–25 min interview when the HM is distant from the day-to-day, when craft language is thin, or when motivators came out generic. Name the interviewee and list exactly what it must cover.

## 5. Outreach essentials

The facts and rules outreach runs on, signed off in the room:

- Company proof points the client signs off for outreach (category leadership, scale, backing) — locked facts, zero recruiting spin
- Role sells, and the honest anti-sells (the soft spots outreach must route around: below-market band, no-remote)
- Comp phrasing rules: exact wording allowed in outreach vs at screening ("salary package with benefits", never "OTE" pre-screen), and when it gets revealed
- Commercially sensitive topics that stay out of outreach entirely, and who reviews grey areas
- Tone constraints and sender identity where the client voiced a preference

## 6. Screening (triage) questions

- The hard gates as askable questions (relocation, office pattern, comp, right-to-work) — drafted live on the call and read back
- The 2–3 signal questions that carry the real bar (mapped to the calibrated criteria)
- Which gates are informational vs rejecting (sponsorship available flips right-to-work to informational)
- Comp wording for the screening email, signed off
- Next-step owner at the client (named TA/recruiter) and their booking link URL
- Battle-card answers: interview process, comp, remote/hybrid, language requirement, project types, team size, visa, growth path, why the role is open
- Rejection reason phrasing per gate (candidate-safe)
- Sign-off channel and owner for the screening questions

## 7. Logistics and ways of working (capture once, used everywhere)

- JD URL (the canonical link; keep it visible, never buried in a file attachment)
- ATS and process tooling; comms channel (e.g. #r-[client] Slack)
- Sign-off owners: comp, screening questions, calibration scores, outreach copy
- Ways of working with the HM: meeting cadence and update format, when to introduce candidates, feedback speed and format
- Supporting files kept with the role record: JD, competition list, blacklist/DNC, prior calibration sheets
- Dates: calibration deadline, persona interview slot, target launch (48–72 business hours from sign-off)
