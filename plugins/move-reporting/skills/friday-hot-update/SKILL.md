# Friday Head of Talent update

Write one end of week update per client for the Head of Talent, with Matt copied. It rolls up the recruiter's hiring manager updates from this week and adds what a Head of Talent wants to see: totals, progress on priority hires, risks to closing and next week's focus.

Work through the steps below one message at a time. Use the interactive question tool where it's available, since recruiters often answer on their phone.

## Step 1: Gather the week's hiring manager updates

When Slack is connected, search the client's Slack for this week's posts from the recruiter titled "end of week update". List the roles you found and ask the recruiter to confirm the list is complete.

When Slack isn't connected or the search comes up short, ask the recruiter to paste the missing updates.

Also ask whether they have last week's Head of Talent update, for the conversion trend. It's optional.

## Step 2: Check the totals

Confirm you have:

- **Calls:** recruiter screens completed this week, across all roles
- **Outbound contacted:** across all roles
- **Submitted:** candidates put forward to a hiring manager this week
- **Roles live**

Take per-role figures from the hiring manager updates and add them up. Ask the recruiter to confirm the totals or correct them. Where their figure differs from the sum, point out the difference.

## Step 3: Ask about priority roles

Ask which roles are this month's priority hires, the ones agreed with the client. Skip this when the recruiter has already said.

For each priority role, give your best reading from the hiring manager updates first, then ask the recruiter to confirm or correct it:

- **Where it stands:** the furthest candidates and when we expect to close
- **Risk to closing:** competing offers, a pay gap, notice periods, hiring manager availability, candidate engagement, counter-offers. Get the person, the issue and the date.
- **What raises the odds:** concrete actions the client can take, such as an HM call with the candidate, flex on start date or package, or faster feedback

## Step 4: Work out conversion

For each role, conversion is submitted ÷ screened. Mark any role under 40% with ⚠️. When last week's update is available, also mark any role whose conversion has fallen for a second week running. Conversion is a week-on-week guide, so keep it to the By role line.

## Step 5: Fill the commentary

Ask for anything the hiring manager updates leave unclear:

- One thing that's working well and worth repeating
- Blockers, named precisely: who, what, by when
- Anything the recruiter needs from the Head of Talent

## Step 6: Write the update

Use exactly this format. It's Slack formatting, so single asterisks give bold.

```
*[Client]: end of week update, [date]*

*Summary:* [One or two sentences across all roles: where things stand and the one thing I need your help with.]

*This week in numbers:*
• [x] calls · [y] outbound contacted · [z] submitted to HM · [n] roles live

*By role:*
• [Role] (priority): [x] screened, [y] submitted, [z]% conversion, [n] outbound
• [Role]: [x] screened, [y] submitted, [z]% conversion, [n] outbound

*Priority roles:*
*[Role]:* [Where it stands, the furthest candidates and when we expect to close]
• Risk to closing: [Who, what, by when]
• What raises the odds: [Concrete actions]

*Blockers:* [Named precisely, with who and by when]
*What's working:* [One thing worth repeating]
*Focus for next week:* [Totals and goals across all roles]
*Actions required from you:* [Decisions and sign-offs]

Have a great weekend!
```

Worked examples are in `references/examples.md`. Read them before writing your first update in a session.

## Rules

- **Numbers:** use only the numbers the recruiter gives you or that appear in the hiring manager updates.
- **Order:** priority roles go first under By role.
- **Risks:** name the person, the issue and the date.
- **Focus for next week:** totals across all roles, with numbers.
- **Summary:** someone who reads only this line knows where the account stands and what's needed.
- **Voice:** plain UK English, the way a recruiter talks to a Head of Talent they work with every week. Write in positive constructions: say what is true and let it stand.
- **Punctuation and words:** use commas, colons or full stops instead of dashes. Use everyday words instead of terms like leverage, robust, seamless, delve or unlock, and skip the "it's not just X, it's Y" reframe.

## Step 7: Hand it over

Give the update in one code block so it copies cleanly. Under it, list anything you flagged, such as totals that disagreed.

When Slack is connected, offer to send it to the Head of Talent and Matt. Send only after the recruiter confirms the recipients and the text.
